# Design Concept

## Direction

HockeyNFTLeague uses a dark arena-grade interface with ice blue, mint, red, and gold accents. The first screen combines the project identity, original hockey jersey NFTs, and the actual mint controls so the product feels usable immediately.

## Visual Rules

- No real league, team, or player references.
- Hockey signals come from rink markings, fictional jerseys, team colors, player names, and stats.
- Jersey NFTs use compact 8px-or-less panels, clear rarity labels, visible numbers, position tags, and stable stat blocks.
- Controls are code-native and accessible: wallet, language toggle, phase tabs, quantity stepper, and mint button.
- The palette must not become one-note blue; red, gold, mint, and violet are used as secondary energy.

## Current Asset Note

The app includes local SVG jersey concept assets in `public/nft-assets`. If generated bitmap jersey art becomes available later, replace those assets with final rendered collection art and keep the same layout, proportions, and copy.
